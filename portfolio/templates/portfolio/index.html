The Origin Story

From construction sites to cloud infrastructure.

Different industries. Different tools. The same instinct to understand how things fit together.

CHAPTER 01 / THE FOUNDATION

Civil Engineering came first.

Learn to build things that stand.

I began with a Diploma in Civil Engineering and practical site experience. Working in Kerala and Saudi Arabia taught me about coordination, planning, deadlines, responsibility, and the real-world consequences of getting the details wrong.

The lesson: Good results depend on solid foundations and attention to detail.
CHAPTER 02 / THE TURNING POINT

Curiosity changed direction.

From physical structures to software systems.

My interest gradually shifted towards programming, automation, Linux, and cloud technology. I started learning Python and Django, then explored the infrastructure and delivery systems behind modern applications. It wasn't a shortcut; it was a decision to start learning a new field from the ground up.

The lesson: A career change doesn't erase your previous experience. It gives you a different foundation to build on.
CHAPTER 03 / THE BUILD

Learning by building.

Less watching. More doing.

I began working on practical projects involving Docker, Jenkins, Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, Helm, Argo CD, security scanning, and observability. I've built CI/CD workflows, experimented with GitOps and progressive delivery, and learned to troubleshoot by working through real failures.

The lesson: Documentation is useful. Hands-on experience teaches you what the documentation couldn't predict.
CHAPTER 04 / RIGHT NOW

An engineer still in progress.

Building towards a career in DevOps.

Today, I'm focused on becoming a strong Junior DevOps Engineer. My goal is to deepen my understanding of cloud infrastructure, automation, secure delivery, reliability, and the systems that help development teams ship software with confidence.

The lesson: I'm not claiming to know everything. I'm committed to learning, building, and getting better.

The Question Everyone Asks

Worried about AI taking over?

Not particularly. I've got a few things going for me. And no, "turn it off and on again" isn't the whole plan.

Bro, I use AI. This Portfolio is Vibecoded!.

I learn, adapt, experiment, and use the tools available to me. AI can generate code, suggest infrastructure, and help troubleshoot. Brilliant. I'll take the assistance.

But someone still needs to understand the problem, question the output, secure the infrastructure, handle production failures, and take responsibility when things go sideways. I'm learning to be that person.

And if the machines eventually decide they're done with us? Relax. I've got other skills. 😌

Different skills. Same survival instinct.

SKILL SET 01 · KITCHEN OPS

The Porotta Engineer. 🫓

I can cook, and I can make porotta. When the AI economy crashes, I won't be staring at a chatbot asking how to boil water. I'll be making food while everyone else tries to prompt dinner into existence.

Incident response? I've handled a burning stove. I know when to act before things get crispy.

SKILL SET 02 · CIVIL ENGINEERING

The Foundation Guy. 🏗️

I have a Civil Engineering background and real site experience. I understand design, construction, supervision, and the difference between something that looks good on paper and something that actually stands.

I can coordinate work, solve practical problems, and negotiate with people who insist their measurements are "approximately correct." Good luck automating that entire conversation.

SKILL SET 04 · AGRICULTURE

The Farmer. 🌱

At the end of the day, humans still need food: protein, fibre, vitamins, and all the other things that keep us alive and functional. Software can't replace the need to grow, source, and prepare them.

So yes, farming is on the list. If the cloud goes down, I'll appreciate the original kind of infrastructure: land, water, sunlight, and a harvest that doesn't need a deployment.

I'm not preparing to fight AI. I'm learning to work with it, building real-world skills, and keeping more than one door open.

DevOps by ambition. Engineer by training. Cook by appetite. Artist by instinct. Farmer, if necessary.

AI takeover? Bro, I'm diversified. 🤝
